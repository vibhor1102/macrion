# Quick Start Guide

Welcome to **Macrion**! This guide walks you through downloading the app, installing it on your phone, and running your first automated tap in under two minutes.

---

## 1. Download & Install

Official APK releases are available directly on GitHub:

👉 **[Download Latest Macrion Release](https://github.com/vibhor1102/Macrion/releases)**

Under the **Assets** section of the latest release, choose the file matching your device:

| Download Option | Which device is it for? |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`...-arm64-v8a.apk`** | **Recommended.** Choose this for almost all modern Android smartphones and tablets. |
| **`...-universal.apk`** | Choose this if you are not sure which processor your device uses, or for older phones. |
| **`...-x86_64.apk`** | Choose this if you are running Macrion inside an Android emulator on your PC (such as BlueStacks, LDPlayer, or Android Studio). |

After downloading, tap the file in your notification bar or downloads folder and tap **Install**.

::: tip Note for Android 13+ Users
If Android prevents you from enabling Accessibility with a message saying *"Restricted setting: For your security, this setting is currently unavailable"*, open your phone's **Settings → Apps → Macrion**, tap the **three dots (⋮)** in the top-right corner, and tap **Allow restricted settings**.
:::

---

## 2. Create Your First Scenario

Open the **Macrion** app. On the main screen, tap the **`+` (New Scenario)** button in the bottom right corner.

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src="/screenshots/scenarios-list.jpg" alt="Macrion Scenarios List" class="phone-screenshot" />
</div>

In the **New scenario** dialog that slides up:

1. Enter a name for your automation (for example, *Upgrade Hero by 10 levels* or *Simple Tapper*).
2. Tap the **Simple** card (*the regular auto-clicker experience*).
3. Tap the **Save** button in the top-right corner of the dialog.

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src="/screenshots/new-scenario-dialog.jpg" alt="New Scenario Dialog" class="phone-screenshot" />
</div>

Your scenario is now ready and appears on your home screen!

---

## 3. Place Your Click Target & Start Tapping

1. On your scenario card, tap the circular **Play / Launch (▶)** button.
2. Macrion will minimize, and the **Floating Control Panel** will appear docked on the side of your screen.
3. Open the game or app you want to automate.
4. Tap the **`+`** button on the floating sidebar to drop a circular click target on your screen.
5. Drag the target marker so the center white dot sits directly over the button you want to tap.

<div class="screenshot-frame">
  <img src="/screenshots/overlay-active.jpg" alt="Floating Overlay with Click Target" class="phone-screenshot" />
</div>

6. Tap the **Play (▶)** button on the bottom toolbar. Macrion is now tapping for you!
7. Tap **Pause (⏸)** at any time to pause, or tap the back arrow (**←**) on the side panel to close the overlay and return to the app.
