Macrion is a free and open-source Android automation app. Create scenarios that react to what is on screen, then perform configured taps, long presses, swipes, text input, and system actions.

Use image, text, number, and colour conditions to make automation respond to screen state. Build simple position-based auto-clickers or more advanced flows with timers, counters, cooldowns, broadcast triggers, and external automation-app integration. Macrion can import and export scenario backups, including compatible Klick'r exports where supported.

Macrion requires Accessibility, screen-capture, and display-over-other-apps permissions to automate other apps. Grant them only when you understand and trust the scenarios you run.
